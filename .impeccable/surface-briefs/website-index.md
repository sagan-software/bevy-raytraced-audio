# Website index surface brief

## Direction contract

THESIS: Present a Bevy acoustics catalogue led by a low-poly forest sound walk.
Visitors open the 3D scene, then choose 2D, 3D, and stress examples by category.

OWN-WORLD: Use Bevy's example-directory conventions: charcoal surfaces, a
compact category index, direct example links, and two-column image cards. Use
deep pine and moss, weathered stone, amber source lights, and teal acoustic paths.

STORY: The scene shows where a listener can move, where the sound source sits, and when a surface changes the direct path. The visitor enables audio, moves with WASD, follows the path overlay, and then chooses a smaller example by category.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Place the site header, search, and examples heading first. Show
the category index beside the forest image and its title, description, and action.
Start category cards below the featured scene.

FORM: Follow the user's Bevy examples reference and the Vercidium forest-demo
references. The user delegated composition choices on 2026-10-06. Concept seed:
`131eb4ba`. The Bevy catalogue reference controls the page structure.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: Walk around the acoustic surfaces while the Bevy direct path and first-order reflection lines follow the moving listener.

## QUALITY BAR

- The first viewport identifies the examples and exposes the forest action.
- Search and category links lead to five distinct runnable routes.
- Desktop and 390px mobile layouts have no horizontal overflow.
- Keyboard focus is visible on the search field, links, buttons, and forest canvas.
- Each browser route reports WebGL2 failures and enables audio after a user gesture.
- The forest shows listener movement and path updates, and states CPU and reflection limits.

FINISH: Review the site, record the result in `DESIGN.md`, and document the
provenance of every shipped raster image.
