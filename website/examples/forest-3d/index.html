Controls

Click the scene, then use WASD or arrow keys to walk. Hold the left mouse button to orbit and use the wheel to zoom. Press B to toggle paths, F1 to show frame timing, and Escape to release the cursor.

What the paths mean

The green to orange line shows direct transmission. Cyan lines show first-order reflection geometry. Reflection paths are visual response data; they do not add reverb or reflection audio.

Materials

The stone arch and brush screen use different absorption and transmission profiles. Their coefficients affect CPU ray queries and direct sink gain.
